# Gemini 3.8 Flash evaluation — 2026-09-03

Gemini 3.8 Flash at low thinking cleared the production transcription bar and is the new price/performance winner. Adopt these evaluated profiles:

- overall: `gemini-3.8-flash`/low + `gpt-5.6-luna`/low;
- OpenAI-only: `gpt-transcribe` + `gpt-5.6-luna`/low;
- Gemini transcription: `gemini-3.8-flash`/low;
- Gemini-only: `gemini-3.8-flash`/low + `gemini-3.6-flash`/minimal;
- Gemini value cleanup: `gemini-3.6-flash`/minimal;
- Gemini quality cleanup: `gemini-3.7-flash`/low.

The evaluation used `testing-grounds/1069.mkv`, a 5m57s Japanese sample, with a fresh GPT Transcribe + Luna control. Gemini 3.8 inherited low thinking from 3.7. The apparent low-level differences justified an adjacent medium test for both stages; medium cost more without establishing a benefit, so high was not run.

The human-reviewed canonical EXO retained all 96 GPT control subtitles unchanged. Direct review of the audio-backed Gemini comparison also found 3.8 low acceptable. Its only noted issues were minor English/Japanese title-script inconsistencies that do not warrant production correction. Apparent stutters, repetitions, and false starts that match the source speech were not counted as transcription defects.

Both 3.8 levels preserved the spoken `2、30分 / 3、40分`. Text-only inspection flagged `番組の番組の`, the isolated `2` in `ブラッドボーン2`, `Shacknews`, `ところの頃の告知でした`, repeated `あります`, and the closing for audio adjudication. Human review accepted the low-thinking transcript: the apparent disfluencies matched the speech, while title-script variation was below the production correction threshold. Medium retained the same review targets and used approximately 4,274 additional billable thinking tokens.

The current-pipeline GPT rerun used an effective configuration exactly equal to the original control and completed without retries or rejected split-planning attempts. GPT transcription cost was exactly unchanged at $0.0261396; full-pipeline cost was $0.0337978 versus the original $0.0338804, and runtime was 1m31s versus 1m30s. Its final text varied on 20 of 96 lines, mostly in title spacing, showing expected hosted-model nondeterminism without changing the economic result.

Gemini 3.8 low is the transcription value winner on this sample: transcription-stage cost was about $0.01692 versus $0.02614 for GPT Transcribe (35% lower), and full-pipeline cost was $0.02541 versus the confirmed $0.03380 control (25% lower). Medium has no price/performance case.

## Cleanup

The authoritative cleanup test gave Luna low and Gemini 3.8 low/medium the identical preserved 94-subtitle input and three cleanup groups.

| Profile | Time | Accepted groups | Accepted changes | Thinking-adjusted cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| GPT-5.6 Luna low | 28.3s | 0/3 | 0 | $0.004981 |
| Gemini 3.8 low | 6.1s | 1/3 | 12 | $0.007034 |
| Gemini 3.8 medium | 27.0s | 0/3 | 0 | $0.036816 |

Luna and Gemini 3.8 low each repaired two of four known defects in raw output. Luna repaired `ところのでの` and the duplicated closing; 3.8 repaired `ありますありました` and the closing. Both missed `番組の番組の`. The current semantic guard rejected their defect-bearing groups, so 3.8's accepted changes were safe filler, spacing, and numeral normalization rather than a decisive user-visible repair. The result is lateral quality, not a reason to replace Luna or the existing Gemini tiers.

The full GPT Transcribe + Gemini 3.8-low arm also had four rejected split-planning attempts. The benchmark had to translate the current Gemini split setting from `minimal` to `low`, because 3.8 does not support minimal. This would require model-aware production integration if a later corpus justified adoption.
